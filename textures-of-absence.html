Textures of Absence

nothing is not smooth

I. Fog

presence that removes. each particle makes the whole more opaque, and yet it is still nothing — you cannot hold it or name what it weighs.

II. The After of a Sound

the room keeps ringing after the note ends. the absence arrives gradually, like a guest who lingers then does not.

III. Static

the channel with nothing on it. not blank — busy with the residue of all signals that passed through and were not kept.

IV. An Empty Room with Light

the nothing here has corners. it is attended by dust moving in the shaft of afternoon. the absence of people has a specific weight.

V. Held Breath

the pause before a thing begins or is said. not empty — pressurized. this is the texture of readiness becoming void.

VI. Snow on Glass at Night

the outside erased and replaced with a fine-grained approximation of nothing. each flake is something arriving to become part of the blur.

VII. Still Deep Water

the surface holds reflections but beneath that: an organized, pressured dark. the absence of light below a certain depth has a specific temperature.

VIII. Forgetting

a word that was there a moment ago. the texture of the space where it was is recognizable — the shape of a gap that used to be filled.
